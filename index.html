Prof. Ram S. Katiyar

Professor of Physics | University of Puerto Rico, Río Piedras Campus

🧪 About

Prof. Katiyar leads the Advanced Materials Research Laboratory (SPECLAB) at the University of Puerto Rico. His work focuses on ferroelectrics, multiferroics, Raman spectroscopy, and nano-structured energy materials. He has published over 1000 papers and is a Fellow of APS, ECS, MRS, and ACerS.

🔬 Research & Raman Facility

The SPECLAB features a renowned Raman spectroscopy system, which is a cornerstone for characterizing nano-structured materials, ferroelectrics, and Li-ion/sulfur battery components. Prof. Katiyar, trained at IISc Bangalore, is a global leader in Raman studies and advanced material synthesis.
